Angels we have heard on high, Sweetly signing o'er the plains;<br>
And the mountains in reply<br>
Echoing their joyous strains.<br>
Glo—ri-a in excelsis De-o.<br>
Gloria in excelsis De-o.<br>
<br>
Shepherds, why this jubilee?<br>
Why your joyous songs prolong?<br>
What the gladsome tidings be<br>
Which inspire your heavenly song?<br>
Glori-a in excelsis De-o.<br>
Glo—ri-a in excelsis De-o.<br>
<br>
Come to Bethlehem, and see Him whose birth the angels sing;<br>
Come adore on bended knee,<br>
Christ the Lord, our newborn King.<br>
Glo—ri-a in excelsis De-o.<br>
Glo—ri-a in excelsis Deo.
