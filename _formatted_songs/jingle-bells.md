We're dashing through the snow<br>
In a one horse open sleigh,<br>
And o'er the fields we go,<br>
A laughing all the way.<br>
The bells on bob-tail ring.<br>
They're making spirits bright,<br>
What fun it is to ride and sing<br>
A sleighing song tonight!<br>
<br>

Oh what fun it is to ride in a one horse open sleigh!<br>

And soon Miss Fannie Bright was seated by my side.<br>
The horse was lean and lank, Misfortune seemed his lot,<br>
He got into a drifted bank And then we got upsot!<br>
