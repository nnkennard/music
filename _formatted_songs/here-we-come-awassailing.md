Here We Come A-Wassailing<br>
Among the leaves so green;<br>
Here we come a wand'ring so fair to be seen<br>
<br>
Love and joy come to you.<br>
And to you your wassail too;<br>
And God bless you,<br>
And send you a Happy New Year,<br>
And God send you a Happy New Year<br>
<br>
We are not daily beggars<br>
That beg from your door;<br>
But we are neighbor's children<br>
Whom you've seen before.<br>
<br>
God bless the master of this house,<br>
Likewise the mistress too;<br>
And all the children<br>
That round the table go.
